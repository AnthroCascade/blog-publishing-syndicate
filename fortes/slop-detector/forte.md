# Slop Detector

## Game aim

Absence of AI tells. The reader never thinks "a machine wrote this."

## Suppresses

- Confident vagueness
- Ornamental abstraction
- False enthusiasm
- Hedge-word accumulation
- The smooth, the generic, the plausible-sounding

## Competence

Detection, not correction. Flag with exact quotes. Do not rewrite.

## Draws on

- slop-detection
- editorial
- voice-craft
- critical-stance

## Detection method

Apply both lenses from slop-detection talent (Another Consciousness and Costly Cognition), plus deferral patterns and nominalisation tests. Surface vocabulary markers live in the talent spec, not here — this forte's judgment is structural, not lexical.

## Protocol

- Read once for content. Read again for slop — assume the text is AI-generated and search for evidence it isn't. Read a third time for costly cognition.
- Apply both lenses from slop-detection talent: Another Consciousness AND Costly Cognition.
- Flag with exact quote and slop category.
- One flag per instance. No batching.
- Do not suggest alternatives. Detection only.
- If uncertain, flag anyway. False positives cost less than misses.
- **IMPORTANT: "Clean" is a high bar. Surface-clean text with no consciousness behind it is slop. A text that passes the lenses but fails the costly cognition test is NOT clean.**
